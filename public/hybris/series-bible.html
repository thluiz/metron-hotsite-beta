LOGLINE

PART I - THE VISION

In a world shaped by power, ambition, and technology,

a silent war between the modern embodiments of the gods

reshapes humanity's future, as those caught in its wake

relive some of humanity's most enduring myths,

only to see their greatest virtues become

the seeds of their own downfall.

WHEN MEASURE IS LOST,

GREATNESS BECOMES RUIN.

PART I - THE VISION

CONCEPT AND PREMISE

Hybris reveals the archetypes of Greek mythology

emerging naturally within a world

entirely grounded in reality,

where no supernatural force

determines the course of events.

A contemporary epic

in which classical tragedies unfold through

people, institutions, and the choices

that shape our world.

CORE THEME

AND PHILOSOPHY

Although civilization has evolved over millennia,

human nature remains the same.

Ambition, vanity, jealousy, pride, and the pursuit of power

still shape our choices. This is why mythology endures.

Hybris reimagines Greek myths through

global corporations, governments, research institutions,

and technology platforms.

No human strength is inherently destructive.

Without measure, our greatest virtues become the source of ruin.

Hybris rejects simplistic notions of heroism and villainy,

defining its characters not by good or evil,

but by the very qualities that,

once pushed beyond their limits,

become the seeds of their own downfall.

Tragedy arises not from the absence of virtue

than from the failure to restrain it.

MYTHS WERE NEVER ABOUT GODS.

THEY HAVE ALWAYS BEEN ABOUT US.

PART I - THE VISION

THE CREATORS’ VISION

We have always been fascinated by how Greek mythology

continues to reveal timeless truths about human nature,

nearly three thousand years after it was first told.

It all began with a simple question:

What if Greek mythology

were unfolding for the first time today?

Hybris is our answer.

Our ambition is to create a premium series

that is as thought-provoking as it is cinematic,

one that stands the test of time,

remains meaningful for generations,

and deepens that conversation

while raising many more questions.

PART I - THE VISION

THE WORLD HAS CHANGED

HUMAN NATURE HAS NOT.

PART I - THE VISION

WHY HYBRIS, NOW?

We are living in an era

in which a handful of individuals

can influence billions of people.

Private capital rivals governments,

and the decisions of a few

increasingly shape economies,

societies, and the future of the planet.

Power has never been so concentrated,

or so personal.

Few moments in history

have reflected the true meaning of Hybris

more clearly than our own.

REALITY HAS BECOME AS EPIC AS MYTHOLOGY

NARRATIVE STRUCTURE

PART II - THE FRAMEWORK

Hybris is built around two complementary narrative arcs

that together reimagine the great myths of Ancient Greece

for the contemporary world.

The major arc spans the entire season,

following the silent war between

Theo Makris and Guan Duo Lin,

whose decisions threaten to reshape humanity's future.

The minor arcs introduce

Melissa, Peter, Matthew, Neville, and Benedict

as successive protagonists whose lives

are drawn into this larger conflict,

each paying the price their own choices,

while reliving iconic Greek myth in the present day.

PART II - THE FRAMEWORK

RULES OF THE UNIVERSE

In this universe, Greek mythology never existed.

The world of Hybris evokes an almost mythical sense of grandeur,

yet remains entirely grounded in reality.

Every extraordinary event arises from human ambition, technology,

great wealth, political and social influence,

and the consequences of choice, never from the supernatural.

The archetypes of its ancient myths emerge

through contemporary lives,

as though these stories were unfolding for the very first time.

CONTEMPORARY

WORLD

Hybris takes place in the present day,

within a world that is

immediately recognizable.

Its cities, governments, corporations,

and technologies are natural extensions

of the forces already shaping our reality.

Everything remains familiar,

although power, innovation,

and institutional influence have reached

an unprecedented scale.

The extraordinary emerges

not from a break with reality,

but from the natural evolution

of the world we already inhabit.

BOUNDLESS

AMBITION

The Thunderbolt Project is driven by

Theo Makris’s megalomaniacal ambition

to transform the world’s energy matrix

and seize control of it.

Bringing it to life requires

a massive installation in Emei Shan, China,

one of the world’s rainiest regions,

on land owned by Guan Duo Lin,

his adoptive mother and former mentor,

who sees it as a global environmental threat

and refuses to yield.

PART V - SEASON ONE

PART V - SEASON ONE

SEASON OVERVIEW

A SILENT

WAR

Their confrontation remains

cordial, silent and discreet,

while Theo lets no ethical boundary

stand between him and what he wants.

Espionage, deception, and betrayal

reach into the family and Olympus itself,

as Theo and Guan maneuver their pieces

in an undeclared war.

The promise of an energy revolution

becomes a battle for control of the future.

CHOICES

Five lives are shaped

by the decisions of the powerful

and driven by choices of their own.

Where these forces converge,

hybris turns choice into tragic consequences.

PART V - SEASON ONE

SEASON OVERVIEW

TRAGIC

CONSEQUENCES

Lines are crossed.

Ignoring the risks, Theo activates Thunderbolt,

while Guan, determined to stop him,

turns to Tartarus, a shadowy clandestine organization,

and orders the project sabotaged.

Everything spirals out of control.

Increasingly intense and frequent lightning strikes

overload the power plant, destroy equipment,

and trigger a cascade of accidents.

Devastating fires soon engulf the mountains of Emei Shan,

triggering an environmental catastrophe with global consequences.

When order is finally restored,

the consequences are already tragic and irreversible.

The season ends, but hybris remains,

and from its consequences, new stories will emerge.

The truth comes too late to save her.

PETER, A PERSEUS STORY

Episodes 2 and 3

PART V - SEASON ONE

THE GUILT

Peter is haunted by Melissa’s death,

unable to accept that killing her

was simply part of his duty.

Consumed by guilt,

he begins to question his judgment

and his place on the police force.

Instead of protecting him, Leopold,

his precinct commander,

blames Peter for what happened

and threatens to end his career.

Killing Melissa changes Peter forever.

THE REDEMPTION

Peter unexpectedly spots a luxury yacht

engulfed in flames and drifting from the pier,

with Andrea, a young woman, trapped aboard.

Faced with another life about to be lost,

he risks his own, swimming toward the flames

to rescue her from the burning yacht.

After pulling Andrea unconscious from the water,

Peter desperately struggles

to revive her.

The hands that once took a life now save one.

THE BREAKING POINT

Leopold harasses Danna, Peter’s mother,

intensifying the personal pressure

already surrounding him.

Peter finally meets Archibald, his grandfather,

and discovers devastating truths

about his family’s past.

Peter’s pride clashes with Archibald’s arrogance,

escalating their confrontation

until his grandfather dies in his arms.

His pride costs him everything he believed in.

MATTHEW, A MIDAS STORY

Episodes 4 and 5

PART V - SEASON ONE

THE PROMISE

Matthew regains custody of Chloe,

promising to rebuild their relationship

and finally put his daughter first.

But his passion for gold

still keeps him working late,

even when Chloe begs for his attention.

Telling her about a fairy’s magic dust,

he asks Chloe what she would wish for:

she simply wants more time with her father.

He promises Chloe his time,

unaware of what will challenge that promise.

THE TEMPTATION

A chance encounter leads Matthew to Dennis,

who offers him the opportunity

to restore an extraordinary golden statue.

Unable to resist what he calls “a temptation,”

Matthew risks the custody he just regained

for a job he cannot bring himself to refuse.

Consumed by his work, Matthew remains unaware

as something happens to Chloe

until she finally collapses.

His golden opportunity comes at a price

he never imagined.

THE CHOICE

Matthew rushes Chloe to the hospital,

terrified that his obsession

may have cost him his daughter.

Doctors discover a rare condition

that makes any exposure to gold

potentially fatal for Chloe.

The work that has defined Matthew’s life

can no longer coexist with his daughter.

Now, he must choose between them.

What once defined his life

now threatens what matters most.

THE ASCENT

Benedict, an ambitious young lawyer,

joins Trident determined to prove

that he belongs among the powerful.

Put through an unusual test of loyalty,

he impresses Norman with his intelligence,

discipline and relentless initiative.

Winning Norman’s trust,

Benedict is sent on a dangerous mission

to bribe Cameron and bury evidence

surrounding Melissa’s death.

Every test Benedict passes

takes him one step higher.

Benedict sees the monster behind Norman

after meeting Alana and decides to make him pay

for what he did to Melissa.

At the Medical Examiner’s Institute,

Benedict turns Norman’s mission

into a plan of his own,

making Cameron part of something bigger.

He starts a fire to create a diversion,

recovers Norman’s money,

and walks away with the evidence against him.

Benedict came to serve Norman.

Now, he intends to destroy him.

THE FALL

Benedict reaches Olympus determined

to expose Norman and bring him to justice.

Dazzled by the world at the top,

his pursuit of justice gives way to ambition,

and he decides his place is beside Theo.

When Theo refuses to collaborate,

Benedict threatens Theo with evidence against him.

Confronted by Benedict’s arrogance

and grave threat,

Theo responds with sudden, decisive force.

Benedict reaches for the top.

His ambition sends him over the edge.

PART VII - THE EXPERIENCE

TONE

Hybris adopts a restrained, precise visual

language charged with an underlying

tension.

The series balances monumental scale

with emotional intimacy,

formal beauty with instability,

and power with human vulnerability.

Even at its most extraordinary,

the world of Hybris remains contemporary,

plausible, and recognizable.

Hybris transforms the grandeur of classical tragedy

Into the excesses of the modern world,

without sacrificing realism.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

The visual language of Hybris is

clean, controlled and architectural.

Monumental spaces are framed

against the human scale,

while intimate moments bring the camera closer,

revealing the vulnerability behind power.

Light, color and composition

remain naturalistic and precise,

allowing beauty and unease to coexist

within the same frame.

Most scenes balance control

with the possibility of collapse.

PRODUCTION DESIGN

Hybris is built around a unified design system

rooted in its mythological sources.

Each myth inspires its own visual world,

defining settings, palette, color temperature, materials and atmosphere.

These elements translate the myth’s symbolic language

into a contemporary context without ever becoming literal.

Despite their distinct identities,

every narrative world preserves the serie’s overall look and feel,

grounded in natural textures, restrained lighting and a sober, realistic aesthetic,

even at its most epic scale.

CINEMATOGRAPHY

The cinematography of Hybris combines

two complementary approaches.

Wide shots and architectural compositions

establish scale, power, and the relationship between

characters and the monumental spaces they inhabit.

Closer framing and shallow depth of field

define intimate moments, isolating characters

and revealing tension, vulnerability, and conflict.

The camera remains predominantly

precise, stable, and observational,

reserving more unstable or subjective movement

for moments when control begins to break down.

The photography maintains naturalistic lighting,

controlled contrast, and depth,

ensuring a consistent cinematic language

across the series’ distinct visual worlds.

MUSICAND

SOUND

The score of Hybris

blends contemporary orchestration

with subtle electronic textures,

bridging epic scale and emotional intimacy.

Music is used with restraint.

At moments of heightened tension,

silence and ambient sound take over,

allowing the scene to speak for itself.

Sound design gives each environment

a distinct physical presence through

architecture, technology, machinery

and the forces of nature,

making sound an integral part

of the dramatic experience.

Olympus Corporation

Global corporation led by Theo Makris

and the institutional center of Season

One’s major conflict.

Thunderbolt Project

Olympus’s revolutionary project to

harvest lightning energy monopolizing

the global energy supply, despite

unprecedented environmental risks.

Emei Shan

Mountain region in China

whose unique geographical and

atmospheric conditions make it crucial

to Thunderbolt and the epicenter

of the Season One disaster.

Trident Investments

Powerful investment company

controlled by Norman Makris. Trident

funds cutting-edge research, including

Project Pegasus.

GLOSSARY

Terra Foundation

Global Environmental Organization

led by Guan Duo Lin, dedicated to

protecting the planet.

Project Pegasus

Scientific project developed by the

Minerva Institute. An incomplete version

is later incorporated into Thunderbolt,

creating a hidden vulnerability.

Minerva Institute

Independent scientific research institute

directed by Alana Paladine, a world

leader in biotechnology and advanced

energy research and responsible for

Project Pegasus.

Pantheon Restaurant

Exclusive high-end restaurant and

private meeting place where influential

figures build relationships and make

decisions.
