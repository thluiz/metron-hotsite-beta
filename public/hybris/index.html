SYNOPSIS

Hybris follows the escalating conflictbetween Theo Makris and Guan Duo Lin, a war fueled bymanipulation, espionage, and family betrayal.

As their rivalry reshapes the world,five ordinary people are drawn into contemporary reimaginingsof the most iconic myths of Ancient Greece.

Their paths converge into a single tragedy.They all fall not because of their weaknesses,but because of the excess of their greatest virtues.
